Message from the Chairman

Welcome to the official website of Kayunga District Youth Council. As Chairman, I'm honored to lead this platform for young people in Kayunga to voice their aspirations and contribute to our district's development.

Our mission is to engage all youth across Kayunga District - rural and urban, educated and non-educated - ensuring everyone can realize their potential. We empower youth to be leaders of change by ensuring their perspectives are integrated into district policies and development plans.

Join us in building a prosperous Kayunga driven by the strength and potential of its youth.

Hon.BEINOMUGISHA MABLE
District Youth Chairman
