Key Programme Areas

Our strategic focus areas for youth empowerment

Priority Programme Areas

Performance and progress will be measured in substantive program areas as reflected in our strategic investment plan. Our key programmes are designed to address the most pressing needs of youth in Kayunga District.

Youth Employment Initiative

Creating employment opportunities for young people aged 18-35. This program provides vocational training and job placement services.

Women Entrepreneurship Fund

Supporting women-owned small businesses with micro-finance and business training in Kayunga District.

Youth Digital Skills Training

Free computer and digital literacy classes. Training in Microsoft Office, internet usage, and basic programming.

Agricultural Extension Program

Modern farming techniques for local farmers. Free training, seeds and agricultural inputs.

Youth Sports League

Annual football and athletics competition. Opportunities to compete at regional and national levels.
